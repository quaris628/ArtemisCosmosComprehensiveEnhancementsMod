These are Quaris's notes for replacing the Cosmos gui's default audio with the Artemis 2 gui audio.
If you find a problem with how they're mapped, or otherwise want to improve on them somehow, etc., hopefully these notes help.

Rough descriptions of the numbered beeps, for reference

	Artemis 2		Cosmos
	testIUSound#		beep#
0*	lo hi me
1*	hi me lo		lo hi
2*	lo me hi		lo hi lo - lower than v
3	hi me hi		lo hi lo - higher than ^
4*	me1 lo (me2) hi		me1 lo hi (me2)
5*	me lo hi		hi me lo me
6**	(hi) lo hi lo		lo me hi
7				hi me lo
8	lo (me) (hi)		hi lo me
9*	hi (lo) (me)		hi lo - same as v
10*	trill up? neutral?	hi lo - same as ^
11	trill up?		me
12*	trill up? neutral?	hi me (lo)
13*	trill down		hi lo - lower than 9 and 10
14* 	lo (me?) hi		lo hi me
15*	trill, neutral?
16*	hi lo hi
17*	hi (lo) hi
18	trill, up? neutral? down?
19*	trill down
20	trill up? neutral? down?
21**	trill down - similar v
22	trill down - similar ^
23***	lo hi
24*	trill down - similar to 21/22, especially 21
25*	hi (me) lo
26*	hi lo (hi)
